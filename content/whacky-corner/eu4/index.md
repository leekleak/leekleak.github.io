Europa Universalis 4 is the ultimate power fantasy

I really really really like Europa Universalis 4. It tickles my brain in all the right ways and I just can't stop playing it.

Admittedly, that does make me sound like an addict. Don't get me wrong, while it's my most played game on Steam by a good margin, I'm not one of the crazies with thousands of hours.

Not for the lack of trying though 😅.

# Achievement hunting
My gaming "career" started in 2017 when I bought myself a Switch. While I had played a couple games on my uncle's Xbox One (AC Black Flag & Unity), this was the first time I had my own console.

The problem was... I was a child and I had just spent all the money I had saved up over the years on the console, leaving nothing for new games.

Fortunately, since I made the purchase around my birthday, as a present, my parents bought me Breath of the Wild.

That would become the only game I had for a loooong time. Keep in mind that this was the first year of Switch. There were pretty much no free/freemium titles on the eShop, no Fortnite to consume the child's attention, no Warframe, no Brawlhalla.

Having BotW and nothing else forced me to make the most of it. I decided to 100% it.
I had the time, I loved the game. Why not?
Well...
The Korok seeds.
There's 900 of them. Hidden all over the massive map. 900. **900.**

But I did it! By the end I had a playtime of something like 260 hours, but I did it! I got the golden poop just in time before Christmas where Mario Kart 8 awaited me.

I would go on to 100% MK8 as well, unlocking everything and beating all the dev ghosts in time trials.

The same would happen with Super Mario Odyssey. I got all the moons available in the world and then grinded the cloud level in Bowser's Kingdom to get obscene amounts of money to buy even more moons from the shops.

I hope the pattern's clear by now. Due to the fact that I just didn't have many games and the fact that the games I had were very very good, I got into the habit of 100% each and every one of them. That's despite Nintendo purposefully not having any kind of achievement system!

As I moved from Switch to Steam, the habit remained. Every game I played I tried to complete. Either fully or as much as possible.

Queue EU4 with its 373 achievements. This is probably the only game I played where achievements are not just a side objective the devs throw in, but an entirely valid playstyle to base your runs upon. Granted, you don't have to, but I sure as hell did!

It crossed my love for achievements with achievements that are actually very fun to aim for and that was one hell of a drug.

# The power fantasy
Strategy games are amazing. Civ6, XCOM, BTD6 are all phenomenal. Hell, the 1 month Diplomacy campaign I hosted is probably the best gaming experience of my entire life. What make EU4 different though, is that it's just not really hard. 

Don't get me wrong, it's certainly overwhelming. You won't become mediocre without spending at least 100-200 hours, but that's the point!

The problem with strategy games that require... actual strategic thinking, is that they are often quite hard. I know! What a surprise! 

All of the games I mentioned earlier require you to think and make actual strategies and decisions on how to win, whereas in EU4 by far the largest factor is just how many game mechanics you understand.

Now. I am aware that all of that sounds like a critique, but it's actually the opposite. That's actually what makes EU4 such a relaxing and fun experience to me!
